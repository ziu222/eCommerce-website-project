A shop that answers when you ask.

EC SHOP brings phones, laptops, audio, cameras and gaming gear from independent sellers into one cart. We review every seller and every listing, and we keep a team on hand for the moment something is unclear.

How it works

  1. 1

    Sellers are reviewed

    A shop is approved by our admins before it can sell, and each product is checked before it appears in the catalogue.

  2. 2

    One cart, many shops

    Buy from several sellers in a single checkout. Each seller gets their own order, so you can follow and cancel them separately.

  3. 3

    Pay your way

    Use your wallet balance or pay on delivery. Prices are confirmed again at checkout, and a cancelled wallet order is refunded.

Support, in two steps

Start with the assistant. It answers from our shipping, returns and payment policies, and can compare two products side by side. If it cannot help, leave a message for our staff and they reply in the same chat.

We answer in English and Tiếng Việt.
